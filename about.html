Who We Are
The Initiative

The Avengers Initiative was the brainchild of Director Nick Fury of S.H.I.E.L.D. — a bold idea to bring together a group of remarkable people, see if they could become something more.

Together, they've defended Earth from alien invasions, rogue AIs, and threats from across the multiverse. United not by chance, but by purpose.

Every hero brings something irreplaceable. Every battle shapes who they are. Every sacrifice reminds us what they fight for.
